Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

NTUV Care ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and mobile app to book AC service, repair and copper piping work, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Customers: name, mobile number, service address, pincode, city, state, optional GST number, AC details, service date/time, and your location if you choose "Use my exact location (GPS)" or paste a Google Maps link.
  • Technicians: name, mobile number, email, addresses, photo, service pincodes, live location while the technician panel is open, bank / UPI details for commission payouts and the documents you upload.
  • Payments: payment ID and status. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's secure page – we never see or store them.
  • Feedback, complaints and messages you send us.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm your booking, assign the nearest technician and send booking updates by SMS, WhatsApp, email or app notification.
  • To prepare invoices, record payments, handle complaints and improve our service.
  • To pay technician commission and maintain accounts as required by law.

3. Sharing

Your name, mobile number and address are shared only with the technician assigned to your booking. Technician name, photo and mobile number are shown to the customer they serve. We do not sell or rent your personal data. We use trusted service providers to run the service: Google (Sheets, Drive, Gmail) for storage and email, Razorpay for payments and an SMS provider for messages.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in our Google account and access to the office panels is protected by login. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. How long we keep it

Booking and invoice records are kept as long as needed for service, warranty, complaints and tax/accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete other personal data at any time.

6. Your choices

  • You can view and edit your details in My Account → My Profile.
  • To correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent, write to us at the email below.
  • The website/app stores small data on your device (for example to keep you logged in). Clearing your browser/app data removes it.

7. Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
