Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: cancel within 30 minutes of booking and your advance is refunded. After 30 minutes it is not refunded. The advance is also refunded if the technician does not come on the booked date, if NTUV Care cancels your booking, or if you were charged twice. Other payments are not refundable.

1. Cancellation policy

  • Within 30 minutes of booking: you can cancel from My Account > My Bookings and the booking advance is refunded. The refund request is created automatically.
  • After 30 minutes of booking: you can still cancel, but the booking advance is not refunded.

2. When you get a refund

  • Cancelled within 30 minutes: you cancelled the booking within 30 minutes of booking it.
  • Technician did not come: the technician did not reach you on the booked service date (or the agreed new date).
  • Cancelled by us: NTUV Care or the technician cancelled your booking.
  • Double payment: the same booking, complaint or invoice was charged more than once – the extra (duplicate) amount is refunded in full.

3. When there is no refund

  • Cancellation of a booking by the customer more than 30 minutes after booking, or a change of plan.
  • The visiting charge when service is declined after the technician's visit.
  • Service charges, parts and material once the work is done. If you are not satisfied with the work, raise a complaint from My Account within 30 days and we will check it.
  • A payment that failed but was not deducted from your account.

4. How to ask for a refund

  1. Log in to My Account on our website or app, open My Bookings and press Request Refund on that booking.
  2. Choose the reason from the list and add your remarks.
  3. We check the request within 2 working days. You can see the status in My Account. You can also contact us at Connect@ntuvcare.com or 8448166502.

5. Refund timeline and mode

Once your refund is approved, the amount is sent back to the same payment method you used (card, UPI, wallet or net banking) through Razorpay. It usually reaches your account in 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. A payment made in cash or directly to our bank account / UPI is returned by bank transfer or UPI after verification.

6. Rescheduling instead of cancelling

If the booked date or time does not suit you, you can ask us to change it at no extra cost by calling us or messaging us on WhatsApp. Your advance stays with the booking.

7. Cancellation by us

We may cancel a booking if the address is outside our service area, the customer cannot be reached, or a technician is not available. In that case the full advance is refunded.
